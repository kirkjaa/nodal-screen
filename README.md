# Strain Watch

Early warning for work-related strain injury from markerless 3D motion, built on NODAL M1 cameras. NODAL's entry to the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge (Population Health: Population and Workforce Health Early Warning).

Status: in development, prototype due 20 November 2026.
